RustyGoose quick answers for players joining the server.

What is this place?

This is RustyGoose. We are a hybrid softcore server with both PVP and PVE players.

What does Softcore mean?

Softcore is a type of game mode that is more forgiving towards players. For example; if you die, you will keep 50% of your inventory! For more information on Softcore please go to: https://rust.facepunch.com/news/soft-refresh

Can I choose if I want to be PVP or PVE?

Yes! Simply go to a safe zone (outpost, bandit camp, ranch, barn or fishing villages) and type /flag in chat. If you have team mates they must be online and in a safe zone for you to change.

Can I flag PVP but my team mate stays PVE?

No, all team members must be the same. If one goes PVP you are all PVP.

Where’s the PVP at?

PVP can be anywhere on the map! But specific locations include Launch and some Monuments during the Monument Events.

When Wipe?

Our server wipes fortnightly, with the occasional 1 Week wipe thrown in. Type /wipe in game to find out more.

What is SkillTree?

SkillTree allows you to modify your experience on the server! Whether you want more resources or take less cold damage in the snow, the choices are up to you! Type /st to see the full list of perks available.

How do I unlock more of my SkillTree?

By simply playing on the server! It’s that easy. Everyday rust activities and events will grant you xp to level up fast!

Can people loot the Giant Excavator if I put fuel in it?

No. All of our public quarries are instanced. Meaning only you can retrieve the fuel and loot from them.

What is RP?

RP is the currency you use in /s.

Does RP carry over between wipes?

No.

If you die and you have stuff in your backpack, what happens to it?

Your backpack is like another inventory. If you die, you will lose your backpack, but if you return to where you died, you will be able to retrieve it.
